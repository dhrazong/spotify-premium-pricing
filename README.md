# Spotify Premium price: what does it cost now, which plan fits your household, and is the upgrade worth it? (Full plan table, price history, and regional rates)

Spotify Premium costs **$12.99 a month** in the US for one person, **$6.99** for a verified student, and the first month is **$0** if you have never had Premium before. Two people at the same address pay **$18.99** on Duo; up to six people at one address pay **$21.99** on Family.

Those are monthly prices with no contract, and the figure you actually see depends on the country your account is registered in. The US is not the most expensive market and it is nowhere near the cheapest. This piece works through the current numbers, what changed since 2023, what each tier includes, and where the plans have limits that do not show up in the headline price.

## Which plan should you pick?

Most people searching for the price are one person with one phone, and for them the answer is Individual. The rest comes down to how many people are listening and whether anyone is enrolled at an eligible school.

- **One listener:** Premium Individual, $12.99/month after a $0 first month.
- **Verified student at an eligible accredited college:** Premium Student, $6.99/month. In the US it includes Hulu's ad-supported plan. In the UK it does not.
- **Two adults at the same address:** Premium Duo, $18.99/month. Two Individual plans would cost $25.98, so Duo saves about $7 a month for a couple paying separately.
- **Three to six people under one roof:** Premium Family, $21.99/month, with separate accounts for each member and parental controls.

One detail decides a lot of these cases. On Duo and Family, the 15 hours a month of audiobook listening belong to the plan manager only. Everyone else on the plan gets the music side without the audiobook hours. If two people in your house listen to audiobooks, that single line changes the maths.

## The price has gone up three times since 2023

Spotify's US Individual price was $9.99 when the service launched in the US in 2011 and stayed there for over a decade. Then it moved three times in three years:

- **July 2023:** $10.99 [official announcement]
- **June 2024:** $11.99 [official announcement]
- **2026:** $12.99 [current US page]

That is roughly an 18% increase from the 2023 level, and about 30% from the original $9.99. The Student plan followed the same path, $5.99 to $6.99. Duo went from $14.99 to $16.99 to $18.99, and Family from $16.99 to $19.99 to $21.99.

Why this matters when you are deciding whether to subscribe: the pattern is a dollar or two every year or so, announced with an email to existing subscribers. There is no announced cap and no published schedule. Anyone telling you the current rate is locked in for good is guessing. What you can rely on is that the price you agree to today is what you pay until Spotify announces a change, and that existing subscribers get notified before the new rate applies to them.

The growth in subscribers alongside those increases is worth one number: Spotify had **200+ million** Premium subscribers when it raised prices in July 2023, and around **300 million** as of August 2026.

## What each tier actually gives you, by situation

**One person, one account.** Individual is the whole product. Ad-free listening, lossless audio up to 24-bit/44.1 kHz, offline downloads for thousands of songs (or automatic Offline Backup), the ability to play albums in any order and skip as many times as you like. You also get the listening tools: DJ, daylist, Jam for listening with friends in real time, Blend for taste matching, plus 15 hours a month from the audiobook catalogue.

**Student.** Everything in Individual at roughly half price, with one condition that catches people: you have to be enrolled at an eligible accredited higher education institution and verify that status. Students in the US also get Hulu's ad-supported plan included. Students in the UK pay £5.99 and do not get Hulu. Third-party coverage puts the US student discount at up to four years; the official page excerpt confirms the price and eligibility but does not state a duration, so check your own verification window.

**Two people.** Duo gives each person a separate Premium account, not a shared login, so recommendations and playlists stay personal. Spotify's terms say the two people must live at the same address. Two Individual plans at $12.99 each come to $25.98, so Duo is the cheaper route for a couple, and the gap is about $84 a year.

**Family.** Up to six separate accounts for people at the same address, parental controls covering every member, and Premium benefits for members under 13. At $21.99, Family costs only $3 more than Duo and adds four more accounts plus the controls. The moment a third person joins the household, or there are children who want their own account, Family is the one to look at. The audiobook allowance stays with the plan manager here too.

## Is $12.99 a month worth it?

Judge it against what you would otherwise pay for, and against what the free tier leaves out. Free Spotify gives you the music library with ads, shuffle-only playback on mobile, and no downloads, so no listening on a plane or in a subway tunnel.

Premium removes the ads, unlocks on-demand order and unlimited skips, and adds:

- **Lossless audio up to 24-bit/44.1 kHz**, now listed as a standard Individual feature in both the US and UK
- **Offline listening**, either by downloading tracks or letting Offline Backup handle it
- **Over 700,000 audiobook titles** in the subscriber catalogue, with 15 hours a month included on every plan
- **1.4k fitness classes** through the Peloton partnership
- **Reserved**, which gives access to tickets for select tours. The terms are narrow: limited eligibility, tickets not guaranteed, select tours only, purchase required. Premium does not come with concert tickets.

The audiobook catalogue has grown fast, from over 250,000 titles in mid-2024 to more than 700,000 now. If you already pay for audiobooks separately, 15 hours a month is the part of Premium that most often tips the maths.

## Three things that trip people up before checkout

**The $0 first month is once per person, not once per plan.** Individual and Student both start at $0 for one month, but only if you have not tried Premium before. If you subscribed years ago and cancelled, expect to pay from day one. There is no published way around that.

**Cancelling is easy; getting money back is not documented.** Every plan says "cancel anytime," and your features run to the end of the paid period. What the pages reviewed here do not state is a refund window for a month already charged. If a refund matters to you, ask support before you pay, not after.

**Student eligibility is verified, and the Hulu bundle is US-only.** The discount requires an eligible accredited institution, and the Hulu inclusion is listed on the US page with eligibility conditions attached. The UK student plan is cheaper in local terms at £5.99 but does not include Hulu at all.

## FAQ

**Is Spotify Premium $12.99 a month?**
Yes, for Premium Individual in the US, after a $0 first month if you have never had Premium. Duo is $18.99, Family $21.99, Student $6.99.

**Do students get Hulu?**
In the US, yes. The Student plan currently includes an ad-supported Hulu plan at no extra cost, subject to eligibility. The UK Student plan does not list Hulu.

**Can two people share Duo if they don't live together?**
Spotify's terms for Duo specify two people living at the same address. The plan is priced on that basis.

**Is lossless audio included?**
Yes. Lossless music at up to 24-bit/44.1 kHz is listed among the standard Premium Individual features in both the US and UK.

**How many audiobook hours do I get?**
15 hours a month from the subscriber catalogue on every plan. On Duo and Family, those hours belong to the plan manager only. Other members get the music features without them.

**Does the price go up after the free month?**
The $0 month ends and billing moves to the standard rate shown when you signed up, which is $12.99 a month for Individual in the US. Any later change would be announced separately.
